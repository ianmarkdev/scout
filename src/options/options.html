Career Clipper settings

Connect the extension to your Career Engine app. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Where the app runs, e.g. http://127.0.0.1:8000 or https://career-engine.test. Plain http is only allowed for this computer.

Create one in the Career Engine folder with php artisan app:extension-token you@example.com. It can only save new jobs, and expires after 90 days by default.

Privacy

The extension reads a page only when you click its icon, and sends the job to your Career Engine app only when you press Save. The token is stored in this browser profile; anyone with access to the profile could copy it, so revoke it with php artisan app:extension-token you@example.com --revoke if the computer is shared or lost.
